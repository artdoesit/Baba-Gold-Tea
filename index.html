Royal Cardamom Chai

5 mins Easy 100% Organic

A rich, aromatic, and warming chai infused with fresh cardamom and premium CTC tea. Perfect for starting your day with authentic Indian flavors.

Servings: 2

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups of hot water
  • 1 cup of milk (or oat milk)
  • 2 tsp Princess Elaichi Tea leaves
  • 1 tsp organic sweetener (jaggery/sugar)

Instructions:

  1. Bring water and Princess Elaichi Tea to a rolling boil in a tea saucepan.
  2. Simmer for 3 minutes to extract the full flavor and cardamom aroma.
  3. Add milk and organic sweetener, then bring to a boil.
  4. Strain into your favorite teacup and serve piping hot!

Classic Masala Ginger Tea

8 mins Easy Warming

A robust, spicy, and soothing black tea brewed with fresh crushed ginger and aromatic whole spices for an extra strong kick.

Servings: 1

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of water
  • 0.5 cup of milk
  • 1.5 tsp Princess Regular Tea leaves
  • 0.5 inch of fresh ginger (crushed)
  • 1 piece of cinnamon/clove

Instructions:

  1. Add water, crushed ginger, cinnamon, and cloves to a saucepan and bring to a boil.
  2. Add Princess Regular Tea leaves and simmer for 3-4 minutes.
  3. Pour in the milk and let it boil up twice for a rich texture.
  4. Strain and serve immediately for instant warming comfort.

Golden Amber Brew

10 mins Medium Rich Aroma

A premium, full-bodied golden black tea brewed with Golden Leaf Special, showcasing a rich amber color and sweet honey notes.

Servings: 4

Ingredients:

  • 2 cups of spring water
  • 2 tsp of Golden Leaf Special leaves
  • 1 tsp of raw honey or rock sugar
  • 1 slice of fresh lemon (optional)

Instructions:

  1. Heat fresh spring water until it reaches a gentle boil.
  2. Add Golden Leaf Special tea leaves to the pot and turn off the heat.
  3. Steep covered for 3-5 minutes to let the golden leaves unfurl completely.
  4. Strain into cups, stir in raw honey or rock sugar, and enjoy the pure amber liquor.
