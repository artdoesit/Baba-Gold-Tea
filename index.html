Organic Cashew Matcha Latte

5 mins Easy 100% Vegan

A rich, creamy, and antioxidant-packed latte powered by Milkies Cashew Milk. Perfect for a clean morning energy boost without the coffee crash.

Servings: 2

Ingredients:

  • 1.5 cups of Milkies Cashew Milk
  • 1 tsp Ceremonial Grade Matcha powder
  • 1 tbsp Maple Syrup (optional)
  • 0.25 cup hot water (not boiling)

Instructions:

  1. Sift the matcha powder into a bowl to remove any clumps.
  2. Add hot water and whisk vigorously in a W-motion using a bamboo whisk until frothy.
  3. Warm your Milkies Cashew Milk in a small saucepan, then froth it using a hand frother.
  4. Pour the sweetened Cashew Milk into a mug, then gently pour the frothy matcha on top. Enjoy!

Almond Berry Smoothie Bowl

8 mins Easy High Fiber

Thick, spoonable berry smoothie blended with Milkies Almond Milk, topped with fresh fruit, chia seeds, and granola for a perfect texture contrast.

Servings: 1

Ingredients:

  • 0.5 cup of Milkies Almond Milk
  • 1 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp chia seeds
  • 0.25 cup granola (for topping)

Instructions:

  1. Add Milkies Almond Milk, frozen berries, and frozen banana to a high-speed blender.
  2. Blend on high, using a tamper if necessary, until completely smooth and thick.
  3. Pour the smoothie mixture into a shallow bowl.
  4. Arrange the granola, fresh berries, and chia seeds neatly on top. Serve immediately!

Creamy Corn Chowder

25 mins Medium Hearty

A comforting, rich, dairy-free chowder made naturally thick and sweet by incorporating Milkies Oat Milk, fresh sweet corn, and gold potatoes.

Servings: 4

Ingredients:

  • 3 cups of Milkies Oat Milk
  • 3 cups fresh or frozen sweet corn kernels
  • 2 medium gold potatoes (diced)
  • 1 small yellow onion (diced)
  • 2 cloves garlic (minced)
  • 2 tbsp olive oil

Instructions:

  1. Sauté the onion and garlic in olive oil in a large pot until soft and fragrant.
  2. Add the diced potatoes, corn kernels, and vegetable stock. Simmer for 15 minutes until potatoes are tender.
  3. Pour in the Milkies Oat Milk and bring to a light simmer. Do not boil.
  4. Blend half of the soup in a blender and pour back into the pot for a thick, chunky texture. Season with salt, pepper, and fresh chives.
